Privacy policy

Last updated 3 November 2026

What this application is

A personal integration run by one person for one Gmail account, their own. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What it accesses

With the account owner's consent, it reads Gmail messages and sends mail as the account owner. It requests only the gmail.readonly and gmail.send scopes. It cannot alter mailbox settings, change labels, or delete mail.

Where data goes

The application runs on the owner's own computer. Message content read by the application may be transmitted to the provider of the AI model that powers the owner's assistant, for the sole purpose of answering the owner's own requests. No message content is sent anywhere else.

What is stored

An OAuth refresh token is held in a keyring on the owner's computer. There is no server, no database, no analytics, and no logging of message content to any remote system.

Sharing

Nothing is sold, rented, or shared for advertising, and no data is disclosed to anyone besides the parties named above.

Revoking access

The account owner can withdraw this application's access at any time through the Third-party connections page of their Google account.
